The Verdict

Case 2 produces the largest number by far (~£4.5M combined) but requires you to be contributing £8,000+/month by Year 20. It is a wealth-building machine if income scales with it — but it is a GIA overflow strategy from Year 14, not a pure ISA play.

Case 5 is the most ISA-efficient structure: ramp up in the early years while income is lower, then max the allowance for 25 years. £1.62M terminal, fully sheltered, no GIA needed. Clean and optimal within the ISA wrapper.

Case 4 is the most conservative and realistic: £712,000 at 55, entirely inside the ISA, with a natural income-linked ramp in Phase 1 and a predictable fixed commitment in Phase 2. The £1,000/month top-up from Year 11 is the key decision point — it needs to be affordable sustainably.

If the goal is maximum ISA-sheltered wealth at 30 years without GIA complexity, Case 5 is the optimal structure. If income growth is the constraint, Case 4 is the most executable.
