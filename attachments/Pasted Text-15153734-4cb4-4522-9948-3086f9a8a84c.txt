The Gap Between 7 and 10

The path to 10/10 is not a redesign. The architecture is already at 9. The gap is entirely in process, rules, and scale — three things that cost nothing to build and compound over years.

1. Write the Drawdown Protocol (7 → 8.5)

The single highest-leverage improvement. The Seven Questions tell you what the regime is. The missing piece is a written rule for what to do when the book is losing money inside a regime shift. Without it, the risk is that the machine gets overridden by emotion at exactly the wrong moment — which is Stage 4, when VWRP is down 20% and the temptation to sell is highest.

The protocol needs three trigger levels and three responses:

Yellow (drawdown 8–12% from peak): No action on sleeves. Review Seven Questions. Confirm regime has not rotated. Document the cause→effect chain. Do not add to losers.

Amber (drawdown 12–20% from peak AND 2s10s inverts AND PMI < 50): Reduce VWRP from 30% to 20%. Increase CSH2 from 7% to 15%. Hold SGLN and ICOM. This is the Stage 4 posture.

Red (drawdown >20% from peak AND credit spreads +200bp AND PMI < 45): Reduce ICOM to 5% (demand destruction kills commodities in deep recession). Increase IDTL to 12–15% (Stage 5 — the deflation hedge finally earns). Increase VAGS to 25%. This is the Stage 5/6 posture — the beautiful deleveraging.

Each trigger must be written down before it is needed. A protocol written during a drawdown is not a protocol — it is a rationalisation.

2. Automate Contributions and Rebalancing (8.5 → 9)

Fixed date, fixed amount, automated. The contribution rate is the dominant return variable at this book size — more so than any asset allocation decision. Every month the contribution does not land on schedule, the compounding clock pauses. Every manual rebalance that gets delayed because the market "feels wrong" is a drift that the ±3pp trigger was designed to prevent.

Set the DCA date. Do not move it.

Set a calendar reminder for the rebalance check — not daily, but monthly, aligned with the contribution date. Check drift. Act only if ±3pp is breached.

The IDTL coupon (Dist share class) needs a reinvestment rule: either reinvest manually into the most underweight sleeve within five business days of receipt, or switch to the Acc sibling when the platform lists it.

3. Phase 2 Expansion at £10,000 (9 → 9.5)

At the £10,000 threshold, two additions move the book from a skeleton to a complete All-Weather structure:

UK inflation-linked gilts (INXG or equivalent, 5–7%): Splits the real-rate exposure between USD (ITPS) and GBP (UK linkers). A GBP-base investor has a natural liability in sterling — owning only USD real rates is a structural mismatch that becomes material at scale. Funded by trimming ITPS from 14% to 8–9%.

EM local currency bonds (SEML or equivalent, 5–7%): The genuine fourth uncorrelated return stream. EM local bonds benefit from dollar weakness, EM growth cycles, and carry — none of which are captured by VWRP (which holds EM equities in USD terms) or ITPS. Funded by trimming VAGS from 18% to 12–13%.

These two additions complete the geographic diversification of the bond and real-rate sleeves without touching the equity or commodity structure.

4. Phase 3 Equity Refinement at £50,000 (9.5 → 10)

VWRP's cap-weighted structure means the growth sleeve is structurally ~65% US, ~15% Europe, ~10% EM, with US mega-cap tech dominating the factor exposure. In a Goldilocks or Reflation regime that is fine. In a dollar-weakening, EM-outperforming cycle it is a drag.

Add an equal-weight or value-tilt complement (5–8%): Something like IWVL (iShares Edge MSCI World Value Factor) or WVAL. Funded by trimming VWRP from 30% to 22–25%. This does not change the regime role of the growth sleeve — it reduces the cap-weight concentration within it.

Explicit currency overlay decision: At £50,000, the 75% unhedged USD block is a £37,500 currency position. That is no longer incidental — it is a deliberate bet on USD strength or weakness. Either accept it explicitly (and document it as part of the long-term debt-cycle / debasement thesis) or hedge a portion of VWRP and ITPS. The decision itself is less important than making it consciously.
