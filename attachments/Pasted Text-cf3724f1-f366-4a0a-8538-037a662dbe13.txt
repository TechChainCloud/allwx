The Full Universe Screen — Five Filters, Every Ticker

All five screens ran simultaneously. What follows is the complete synthesis: 30+ tickers across five systematic filters, organised by Principia sleeve fit, with live data, regime scores, and a frank verdict on each. This is the universe the farm has not yet considered.

The Screening Framework

Before the results, the filter that was applied to every candidate:

Minimum track record: 3Y annualised return available (eliminates post-2023 launches with no stress-test data)

Regime coverage: must earn its allocation in at least two of the six historical weathers

Principia fit: must fill a gap in the current book or improve an existing line — not duplicate it

No seventeenth line rule: every candidate is assessed as a replacement or absorption, not an addition

Factor-based ETFs offer low fees, strong liquidity, and high transparency — making them a more cost-effective holding than a group of industry-based ETFs. BOCOM Securities

The Three Highest-Conviction Additions from the Full Universe

If the Principia protocol allows only one new line at a time, pointed at by contributions on the 3rd, this is the priority order:

WNRG LN — The Derrick. Energy equity is the single largest gap in the current book. The farm has broad commodities (ICOM) but no energy equity operating leverage. In every stagflation scenario from 1973 to 2022, energy equity was the top-performing asset class. At 2–3% of the farm, a −40% year (2020 COVID crash) costs 80–120bp. The 3Y annualised return of +43.2% is the highest of any gap-filling candidate. This is the line the farm needs most tonight.

IWVL LN — The Value Engine. The value factor has delivered +34.8% annualised over 3 years in a high-rate regime — exactly the environment where value outperforms growth. If the engine (VWRP) is the farm's largest line at 45%, tilting even 5–10% of that toward value via IWVL would materially improve the farm's regime resilience without adding a new line. IWVL could absorb part of VWRP's weight rather than sitting alongside it.

FLOT LN — The VAGS Replacement. The most actionable quiet farm improvement. FLOT yields 4.17% with 1.67yr duration vs VAGS's 7–8yr duration in a positive stock-bond correlation regime. This is not a new line — it is a direct replacement that removes the biggest structural weakness in the quiet farm. The contribution protocol handles it: point the next month's cash at FLOT, let VAGS drift below target, and do not reinvest VAGS distributions.
